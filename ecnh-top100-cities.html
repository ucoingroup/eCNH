六个必须说清的发现Six findings that matter

1. 五个最高档辖区，只有香港的法律文本没排除人民币1. Of five top-tier jurisdictions, only Hong Kong does not exclude RMB

香港《稳定币条例》覆盖「法币稳定币」，CNH 属于法币；新加坡的单一货币框架只认 SGD 与 G10；美国 GENIUS 法案以美元计价为核心；欧盟 MiCA 对非欧元稳定币设每日交易上限；日本则要求与日元或持牌信托挂钩。这不是偏好问题，是法律定义问题。Hong Kong's ordinance covers fiat-referenced stablecoins and CNH is fiat. Singapore's single-currency framework recognises only SGD and G10. The US GENIUS Act centres on USD. MiCA caps non-euro stablecoins by daily volume. Japan ties issuance to JPY or licensed trusts. This is legal definition, not preference.

2. 「加密友好」与「对 eCNH 友好」是两张完全不同的名单2. "Crypto-friendly" and "eCNH-friendly" are two different lists

迈阿密、奥斯汀、巴厘岛这类地方对炒币很宽松，但都不具备发行离岸人民币支付代币的法律路径。反过来，香港、阿拉木图这类地方热度不高，制度上却有位置。看这张表时请先确认自己需要哪一种。Miami, Austin or Bali are relaxed about trading but offer no legal path to issuing an offshore RMB payment token. Hong Kong or Almaty are quieter but have structural room. Decide which one you actually need before reading the table.

3. 中亚出现了一个真实的 CNH 代币实验场3. Central Asia has a genuine CNH-token experiment

阿拉木图 AIFC 法域已为离岸人民币代币发出牌照（AxCNH 为公开案例），采用英国普通法体系、独立法院，是 100 个城市里少数「法律上真的能装下 CNH」的地方。缺点是市场深度与银行配套远不如香港。The AIFC jurisdiction in Almaty has licensed an offshore RMB token (AxCNH is the public example), operating under English common law with its own court — one of the few places on this list where a CNH token legally fits. Depth and banking support lag far behind Hong Kong.

4. 欧盟 27 国能进，但做不大4. The EU is open, but capped

MiCA 给非欧元稳定币设了每日 2 亿欧元或 100 万笔交易的上限，超过即须停止发行。这意味着欧洲可以作为一个合规展示窗口，却很难成为主要流通市场。真正做规模，仍要回到亚洲时区。MiCA caps non-euro stablecoins at €200m or one million transactions per day, beyond which issuance must stop. Europe works as a compliance showcase, not a primary circulation market. Scale still lives in Asian time zones.

5. 非洲与拉美的真实需求来自汇款，不是投机5. Real demand in Africa and Latin America is remittances, not speculation

内罗毕、拉各斯、布宜诺斯艾利斯、圣保罗的稳定币使用率上升，驱动力是本币贬值与跨境汇款成本，而不是杠杆交易。这类市场对「锚定人民币」本身没有偏好，但对低成本、可结算的稳定币有刚需——这是 eCNH 最务实的落地场景。Stablecoin usage is rising in Nairobi, Lagos, Buenos Aires and São Paulo because of currency depreciation and remittance costs, not leverage. These markets have no particular attachment to RMB — but a real need for cheap, settleable stablecoins. That is eCNH's most practical use case.

6. 一半以上的城市，星级其实只有 2–36. More than half the list sits at 2–3 stars

100 个城市里，5★ 只有 5 个，4★ 12 个，其余多为「允许但不明确」或「明确禁止」。这不是清单不够努力，而是 CNH 计价代币在全球仍属少数派。把 3★ 当成「可以去做」，是这份文档最容易被误读的地方。Of 100 cities, only five are 5★ and twelve are 4★; the rest are either "permitted but unclear" or "explicitly prohibited". That reflects reality, not a weak list. Treating 3★ as a green light is the easiest way to misread this document.

使用这份清单的三条纪律Three rules for using this list

① 星级不是法律意见① Stars are not legal advice

所有评级都是对公开资料的定性归纳，任何实际展业前须由当地执业律师出具意见。监管状态变化极快，本文所记为 2026 年 11 月可查证的信息。Every grade is a qualitative summary of public information. Obtain a local counsel opinion before any actual operation. Rules move fast; this reflects what was verifiable in November 2026.

② 先确认「发行地」与「展业地」是两件事② Issuance and operation are two different places

在 A 地注册发行主体、在 B 地服务用户，是常见结构；但两边的合规义务都要满足，不能靠「注册在宽松地」规避展业地法律。Incorporating the issuer in A while serving users in B is common, but both sets of obligations apply. A lenient registry does not override the law where you actually operate.

③ 不要用「监管友好」替代「商业可行」③ Do not confuse regulatory openness with commercial viability

有些辖区法律宽松但几乎没有用户、没有银行、没有清算通道。落地成本往往高于在严格辖区合规的成本。Some jurisdictions are permissive but have no users, no banks and no clearing. The cost of operating there often exceeds the cost of complying somewhere strict.
