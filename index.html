背景知识Background

仙女座星系The Andromeda Galaxy

仙女座星系（Andromeda Galaxy，编号 M31）是我们银河系最近的巨大邻居，也是人类肉眼可见的最遥远天体之一。它不仅是天文学研究的里程碑，也是未来银河系命运的关键角色；它的庞大、接近与结构复杂性，使其成为星际文明设定中最常被引用的真实星系之一。The Andromeda Galaxy (M31) is the nearest giant neighbour of our Milky Way and one of the most distant objects visible to the naked eye. It is both a milestone of astronomy and a key player in the Milky Way's far future; its size, proximity and structural complexity make it the most frequently cited real galaxy in interstellar-civilization settings.

离岸华夏币（CNH）Offshore renminbi (CNH)

离岸华夏币（CNH）与美元（USD）的汇率，是指在中国大陆以外的市场上华夏币与美元之间的兑换比率。离岸华夏币指在境外交易的华夏币，通常通过香港、新加坡等国际金融中心流通；岸内华夏币（CNY）则指在中国境内使用的华夏币。离岸汇率受市场供求、国际经济形势、央行政策等多种因素影响，因此与岸内汇率可能有所不同。The CNH/USD rate is the exchange ratio between the renminbi and the US dollar in markets outside mainland China. CNH is renminbi traded offshore, typically through financial centres such as Hong Kong and Singapore; CNY is renminbi used inside the mainland. Because the offshore rate reflects supply and demand, global conditions and central-bank policy, it can differ from the onshore rate.
